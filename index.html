The non-negotiables

The winning formula

Every top seller did these four things. Start here.

01

Talk to camera

Your real voice, conversational — like telling a friend. Spoken reviews convert 3× higher than silent clips.

02

Hook in 3 seconds

Open with a proven line (steal one below). Curiosity, authority, or a real question — not a logo.

03

30–60 seconds

Long enough to hook, demo, prove, and ask. Sub-20s cut-downs barely convert.

04

Offer + “link in bio”

Name the deal (bundle / limited-time) and tell them exactly where to tap.

✅ Do

  • Talk through it in your own words
  • Demo on your face — half-face or before/after
  • Say the shade name (Banana / Translucent / Pink)
  • Answer a real question buyers ask
  • Show the offer + point to the link
  • Clean, polished look & good lighting

🚫 Don't

  • Silent clip riding a trending sound
  • Let on-screen text do all the selling
  • Under 20 seconds with no substance
  • End with no call to action
  • Just hold the product — no demo
  • Rushed, dark, or low-effort footage

Shade cheat sheet

200+ comments couldn't pick a shade. Clear this up on camera.

Banana
Warm or neutral undertones — adds a subtle brightening bake.
Translucent
Cool or rosy undertones — invisible, no flashback.
Pink
Brightening “glow” pick — the cult-favorite for radiance.

Confirm exact shade names/availability with the brand before filming.

Answer on camera

Questions to knock out

If you answer these, you remove the last reason not to buy.

  • Does it cause flashback in photos?
  • Does it crease or cake under the eyes (esp. 40+)?
  • How long does it actually last — all day?
  • How does it compare to Laura Mercier / Huda?
  • Which shade is right for me?
